What is Formula Student?

Organised by the Institution of Mechanical Engineers and held every year at the Silverstone Circuit, Formula Student is an international engineering competition. University students take on the challenge of creating their own race cars, from the first design to the final race.

It is not just about building a fast car. It is about engineering, teamwork and problem-solving, and about putting everything you have learned into practice.

This is where we come in.
S02Who we areENP Algiers

One club, seventy engineers

We are the ENP Racing Team, an engineering club of 70 members at the National Polytechnic School of Algiers. The team brings together students from different engineering fields who share one goal: working together on technical projects, taking part in competitions and developing new ideas.

We started as a Formula Student team, and we became the first team from Algeria to present its own single-seater. Today we also race in electric and underwater robotics competitions.

This season:
what are we working on?

Three projects run in parallel: a faster combustion car, our first electric concept, and a new underwater robotics competition.

01
IC
In development

IC Dynamic

Our goal is a new internal combustion car that surpasses our first edition. Drawing on the lessons from our previous car, we are improving its performance, reliability and overall design. We want a more competitive and more refined vehicle.

02
EV
Design 2027 · Build 2028

EV Concept Class

Our new project: designing a complete electric Formula Student car. The work covers simulations, calculations, technical reports, cost analysis and project management. We aim to complete the design in 2027 and build the physical car in 2028.

03
ROV
New competition

MATE ROV

MATE ROV is a competition where students design and build underwater robots, also called ROVs. The robots complete missions inspired by real maritime work.
